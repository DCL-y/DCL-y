## Research focus

Simultaneous multi-system monitoring of cardiovascular and autonomic regulation by **spiroarteriocardiorhythmography (SACR)**, applied to:

- cardiorespiratory interaction and its physiological / pathophysiological mechanisms
- overtraining and overstrain in elite athletes (functional and non-functional overreaching, OTS)
- arterial hypertension, manual therapy, percutaneous coronary intervention
- physical rehabilitation in military trauma and spinal cord injury
- ultra-endurance physiology; sex differences in cardiorespiratory regulation
